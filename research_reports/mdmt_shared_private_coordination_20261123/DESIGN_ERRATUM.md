# S1 fork count

The named interventions in D2 design §8 and plan 03 §2 specify 19 forks per anchor. Two J2 batch mappings, one J_ACC, one U_ONLY, three private-only controls, and twelve sequential combinations sum to 19. Across two datasets, five seeds and three anchors, the required total is 570 forks. The unstepped anchor and recorded intermediate predictions are additional readouts of these comparisons.

This corrects the arithmetic in design §8. Review and execution use the full set of named interventions and required batch mappings.
