## 1. Research question

Does a real shared-representation Adam update cause heterogeneous changes in the six validation ranking paths of M3oE fixed1? Does updating a private path afterward produce an order-specific ranking benefit, and can that benefit survive 50 epochs of the fixed schedule?

## 2. Hypotheses

- H1. An identical upstream-only update has meaningfully different logit, pair-margin and AUC effects across domain-task cells at multiple anchors and seeds.
- H2. On held-out Validation rows, a private update after a shared update improves ranking beyond an upstream-only control, the reverse order, and joint accumulation.
- H3. A single, frozen scope retains selected-validation Macro AUC gains against all three internal training controls and satisfies the cell protection and final qualification gates.

The 11-22 B study established real/virtual Adam identity and small, uneven local responses on a different intervention. Its pulse and held-out source-selection results did not establish a sustained improvement. A's matched history gains were small on validation and reversed direction across some validation/Test contrasts. Neither result settles D2. Historical references are `26-11-23@9df48737dd6693f892b85db2344cdee76fb05d6f`, its `FINAL_REPORT.md`, `FINAL_REVIEW.md`, `A_CORE_REVIEW.md`, `B_CORE_REPORT.md`, and `artifacts/conditional_history_shared_update_20261122/S5/` scalar receipts.

## 3. Variables changed

Change which partition receives an Adam update, the order of two partition updates, or whether two gradients are applied jointly or separately. S1 compares three private scopes. S2 uses the unique S1 scope. All methods optimize the natural two-task objective.

## 5. Algorithm

For B rows and two labels per row, use native probability BCE with the source FP32 reduction `binary_cross_entropy(p, y, reduction="none").mean(1).mean()`. Preserve this actual reduction order on joint steps. Let A and B be two complete natural batches.

- **Joint step.** Clear gradients to None, run one full forward and backward, and step the single Adam. For a subset step, clear excluded gradients to None just before the native step. An included explicit zero gradient retains native momentum and coupled decay semantics. For excluded parameters, θ, presence/absence of state, m, v and step stay identical.
- **Joint accumulation.** Compute both gradients at exactly the same parameter state and apply `(|A|g_A+|B|g_B)/(|A|+|B|)` once to the joint model. Restricted accumulated controls apply the same gradient to the named subset only. The first EMA tick uses the unchanged online state. The second uses the Adam post-state.
- **Sequential.** U→P runs a U step on A, ticks EMA, then recomputes B's forward and gradients at the updated U and steps P, followed by a second EMA tick. P→U runs the same schedule with partitions reversed. J2 steps jointly and ticks EMA twice.
- **Mechanism readout.** Record the U→P intermediate online prediction after U(A) or U(B), before the P update. Estimate shared response from the anchor to this intermediate state and literal post-U recovery from the intermediate state to the final state. Report the distinct registered endpoint contrasts to accumulated U_ONLY, P→U, J_ACC and J2. The intermediate readout adds evaluation forwards only.

Partition by actual canonical names and verify dependencies. Native gate/tower index is `3*t+d`, yielding `d0t0,d1t0,d2t0,d0t1,d1t1,d2t1`. Domain-major report index is `2*d+t`. Forward takes `[F,B]`, ends with domain indicator, and returns `[B,2]`.

| Partition | Source members and expected functional support |
|---|---|
| U | Embedding, `shared_weight`, `shared_bias`, `slot_weight.*`, `slot_bias.*`, `skip_conn.*`, `star_mlp.*`, `expert.*`. Each slot supports its own domain across both tasks. Remaining upstream paths can support all cells, with embedding rows depending on feature IDs |
| P_CELL | `gate.*` and `tower.*`. Each uses the corresponding cell output. Gate consumes `emb.detach()`: its input values respond to U while gate gradients to that input are cut. Gate parameters remain trainable |
| P_AXIS | `domain_expert.*` for its domain across both tasks and `task_expert.*` for its task across all three domains. Canonical fixed1 off-axis blend coefficients are zero |
| P_FULL | Union of P_CELL and P_AXIS |
| FROZEN/OTHER | Any source nontrainable state, plus metadata for the four plain numeric fusion constants. A fourth trainable group is an S0 blocker |

Autograd may create exact-zero gradients for evaluated but masked or zero-weighted paths. Manifest separately records structural forward dependence, None/zero/nonzero gradients, and optimizer membership. Apply single-cell loss against the native forward for all six audits. Use projection hooks on the original towers to read presigmoid logits and verify source routing. Reuse the source tower hook function where practical.

## 8. Ablations

- S1 at every dataset/seed/anchor uses J2 in AB and BA, J_ACC, U_ONLY, P_CELL_ONLY, P_AXIS_ONLY, P_FULL_ONLY, and both U→P and P→U in AB and BA for each of P_CELL, P_AXIS and P_FULL. That is 18 forks per anchor, 540 over 30 anchors. Use each seed as the independent statistical unit.
- S2 uses J_ORIG, J_ACC, P→U and U→P at the single frozen scope. This is at most 30 new trajectories beyond the 10 S1 ones when J_ORIG identity is preserved. Reverse order isolates the order effect at fixed group clocks. Joint accumulation and canonical joint distinguish the update-count and exposure explanations. No extra scope, controller, EMA search or reweighting is registered.

## 11. Falsification and disposition

- H1 is weakened if cell AUC and pair-margin variation is near measured numerical noise or lacks repeatable structure across seeds and anchors. A common change to logit scale alone does not establish ranking heterogeneity.
- H2 fails its gate if S1 lacks one eligible V_A scope with independent V_B order and J_ACC gains, the gains vanish after averaging AB/BA, anchor replication fails, or AUC/cell protection fails despite lower LogLoss. No V_B-driven replacement scope is allowed.
- H3 fails its validation gate if U→P does not beat J_ORIG, J_ACC and P→U on the registered contrasts, relies on one seed, or breaches the cell guard. A reproducible H2 alongside a failed H3 supports a local effect within this protocol. In the eligible frozen S3 comparison, require at least +.003 Macro AUC versus the strongest comparable published baseline, each cell at least -.001, and positive independent increment versus J_ORIG and J_ACC. A dataset-specific result supports only that dataset.
- When a scientific gate fails, finish the registered report as `COMPLETE_NO_GO_S1` or `COMPLETE_NO_GO_S2`. Source, storage, or matchability failures use `TECHNICAL_BLOCKED` or `PAUSED_RESOURCE`, with no inference about the hypothesis. Final authorized S3 completion, if reached, uses the runbook disposition and separately reports whether algorithm qualification was attained.
