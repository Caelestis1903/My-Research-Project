## 1. Research question

Does a Train-global pairwise loss aligned with six-cell Macro AUC improve selected M3oE ranking on the clean BCE and GA31-R3 D0 BCE backgrounds? Does sharing Gaussian noise within a pair change the gain after the independent-noise method clears its parent comparison?

## 2. Hypotheses

Clean pair supervision improves selected Macro AUC by at least .002 over matched clean BCE. Gaussian pair supervision improves selected Macro AUC by at least .001 over matched GA31-R3 D0 BCE. A difference between the clean and Gaussian pairwise increments may reflect BCE background, pair view, or both. The A2 comparison isolates endpoint noise coupling while holding the Gaussian BCE background fixed.

## 5. Mathematical and algorithmic definition

### Train-global macro6 sampler

For domain `d` and joint state `a = 2*y0 + y1`, construct `G[d,a]` in canonical Train row order. Use the order of v3 `boundary_sampler.CONDITIONS` and keep conditions `i=(d,t,a,b)` with both strata present and opposing labels on `t`. For cell `c=(d,t)` define `ri=(|G[d,a]|/nplus[c]) × (|G[d,b]|/nminus[c])`, `wi=ri/(6 × sum_cell(ri))`, and `qi=.5/|active|+.5*wi`, in FP64. Every cell has mass 1/6 and `wi/qi ≤ 2`. Audited counts are 12 active conditions for MovieLens and 20 for KuaiRand. Missing cell support blocks the dataset before dispatch.

Use the unchanged `boundary_sampler.rng(context, stream)` seed derivation with context `(dataset, seed, epoch, global_step)`. The helper hashes `dataset|seed|epoch|global_step|stream` with SHA-256 and feeds the first eight bytes as little-endian seed to `np.random.default_rng`. Use the stream `macro6_condition` to make one right-sided cumulative-`q` draw and `macro6_pair_indices` for replacement draws of 2,048 positives followed by 2,048 negatives from the selected strata. Neither study arm nor attempt enters these streams. Preserve multiplicity and order. Retrieve features in that order using Train IDs mapped to canonical Train feature offsets. Use SHA-256 over the concatenated little-endian int64 positive and negative IDs as the ordered pair hash. Bind the hash to dataset/seed/step, condition and Train identity in the trace.

For the selected task `t`, take the positive block and negative block of true routed logits and form `margin[j] = zplus[j,t] - zminus[j,t]`. Use `Lpair = (wi/qi) × mean(softplus(-margin))`. Its expectation over the condition and pair draws is the equal-six-cell mean softplus pair risk, a smooth ranking surrogate. Gradients from `0.05 × Lpair` follow the ordinary full-model graph alongside BCE and enter native Adam. A21's scope, importance estimator, clocks and history are specific to B.

### Clean and Gaussian BCE

Use the exact main GA31 source `objectives.losses` scalar path. Clean computes unreduced BCE on the canonical two-task probabilities and uses `real.mean(1).mean()`. G0/GP0 make one D0 view on the same BCE rows where `x[-1] == 0`, retaining input order. If D0 is present, use `(real.sum() + .5 × (augmented_D0.sum() - real[D0].sum())) / real.numel()`. An empty D0 slice takes the original clean path. This is half-loss replacement on both D0 task labels and the original denominator `2*batch_rows`. Explicitly freeze target 0 for both datasets. The generic v3 KuaiRand target 2 and EMA02 `normal()` row-keyed path have different identities.

Use `views.draw_view` and `protocol.view_key` for `GA31-R3|{dataset}|{seed}|train|{global_step}|gaussian|1` and pass `[view]` as `explicit_views` on the existing scalar path. `derive_seed` uses SHA-256's first eight bytes as little-endian followed by the 63-bit mask. `draw_view` uses a device-local `torch.Generator` and one `torch.randn((rows, 16), ...)` per field in order. The MovieLens field order is `user_id, movie_id, gender, occupation, zip, cate_id`. KuaiRand uses `user_active_degree, is_live_streamer, is_video_author, follow_user_num_range, fans_user_num_range, friend_user_num_range, register_days_range, onehot_feat0…onehot_feat17, video_type, upload_dt, upload_type, server_width, server_height, music_type, tag, counts`, in that order. The perturbation is `embedding + kappa × detached_per_row_field_RMS × epsilon`, with κ .05 for MovieLens and .1 for KuaiRand. Domain embeddings and dense features are excluded by the original `perturb_slices` construction.

On GP0 D0 pair steps, draw one independent 4,096-row view from the same helper under `GA31-R3|{dataset}|{seed}|train|{global_step}|gaussian_pairwise|1`. Duplicate row IDs receive separate endpoint draws. Outside D0 the pair forward takes clean input. Base BCE uses its original stream across G0/GP0 and A2. 3. ## 11. Falsification and decision rules

Apply the paired mean gates on each dataset, and require every protected six-cell paired mean to meet −.001. P0−B0 below +.002 defeats the practical clean-ranking claim. GP0−G0 below +.001 or failed cell protection closes the Gaussian follow-up. Report GP0−P0 even when it favors the clean objective, alongside the marginal contrasts and interaction. LogLoss improvement without the AUC gate supports a calibration finding. A2 can show shared coupling adds no practical gain if its mean difference falls below +.001, or lose protection despite a mean gain. Report seed spread and cost in all cases. Phase promotion relies on selected Validation and the frozen method's final practical thresholds apply only to authorized selected Test evaluation. Compare each readout only with its matching parent readout and keep Test sealed under the existing stop, freeze and eligible RAM gates.
