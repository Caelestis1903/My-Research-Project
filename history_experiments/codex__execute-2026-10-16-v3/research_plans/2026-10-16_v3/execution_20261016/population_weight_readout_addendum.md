# Descriptive evaluation weights, before new diagnostic results

Primary endpoints and frozen selection rules remain six-cell macro AUC/LogLoss. For secondary interpretation, distinguish three different averages: (1) six-cell macro; (2) within-cell metrics weighted by actual evaluation-split cell counts; (3) within-cell metrics weighted by the original training domain p, equal across the two tasks. The third matches the target domain mixture of the Sample objective; the second instead describes the observed validation/test mixture. KuaiRand has materially different train/validation domain shares, so these cannot be called the same population metric. Weighted within-cell AUC is not pooled cross-domain AUC. Training-objective-weighted evaluation LogLoss still evaluates a different split, not empirical train loss. No additional weighting is used to select LR/checkpoints or alter the primary endpoint. This addition only prevents a population-mixture interpretation error in the final descriptive tables.
