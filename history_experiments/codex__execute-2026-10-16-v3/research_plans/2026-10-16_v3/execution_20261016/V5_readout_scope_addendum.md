# V5 readout scope — before any V5 training or readout

This records how the already specified WR/PERM and exact-batch ORDER comparisons will be interpreted. It does not change the frozen LR, endpoint, extension rule, or V11 gate. Primary design, implementation and self-review follow start.md §11; independent_review=false.

Every variant inherits its optimizer/seed's finalized baseline budget, with both q arms matched in examples. Any additional operational extension must carry the compared factor set to a common budget, retaining the original fixed50 readouts. Duration is determined by train objective and selection only. All configurations and sampler identities are checked before scientific comparison.

For each optimizer, retain a single common pre-degradation interval across its four seeds, both q arms and all compared sampler conditions (Adam: WR/PERM; SGD: WR/PERM/ORDER). Report each seed's interval separately as well. Use this common interval for contrasts between q gaps and for within-q variant-minus-baseline effects. An optimizer interaction between sampler contrasts requires a further common interval across both optimizers. Fixed5/10/15/20/30/40/50, AULC1..10/20, independently selected and common-selected values remain visible; do not replace the main interval with a favorable endpoint.

Report absolute SN and SB values, their paired gap, within-SN and within-SB interventions, and the paired difference between variant and WR q gaps, with four independent training seeds and their CIs. Selection-row uncertainty remains separate. A CI crossing zero does not establish that a gap disappeared. Apply the existing ±.003 practical region and blocking-key-null extension rule where a null is required for a core conclusion. Report loss, every domain/task cell, actual evaluation-count-weighted and training-p-weighted cell metrics alongside macro AUC. A weighted mean of cell AUCs is not pooled AUC.

PERM preserves the physical-domain sequence, exact quota, objective, initialization and optimizer. It jointly changes coverage, repeat locations and temporal dependence. At fixed exposure E and realized unique rows U, repeated draws R=E−U; this sampler cannot independently identify coverage and repetition. When quota exceeds pool support, PERM necessarily repeats rows. Even if the q gap is attenuated under PERM, the intervention supports this sampler bundle rather than unique repetition necessity. The completed sampler and exposure records must precede a non-identifiability conclusion; no fabricated regression on three domains.

ORDER preserves exact batch row contents, batch sizes and the epoch's multiset, changing only their step order. The gradient functions associated with these batches are unchanged; their realized gradients along moving parameter trajectories need not be equal. Full-training differences identify the effect of the specified order intervention for the paired seeds. They do not establish an arbitrary-order theorem or a unique optimizer-memory mediator. Keep per-step lag1/2/5 gradient direction cosines separate from fixed-theta IID covariance and from centered scalar autocorrelation.

V6/V7 are upgraded only for residual unexplained effects after these comparisons. The V1 geometry probes are fixed-reference configurations; their direct numerical identity to new fixed-SGD checkpoints has been verified, while extrapolation to recalibrated configurations is an inferential limitation. Geometry association cannot by itself identify anisotropy as more explanatory than trace when both change.
