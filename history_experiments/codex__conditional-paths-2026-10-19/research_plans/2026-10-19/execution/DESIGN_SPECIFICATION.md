## Models and neutral interventions

M3oE fixed/original/original_sample reuse the pinned N19 model math. Slot-off sets only effective input-weight contribution to zero, retaining biases/STAR MLP/Skip/experts/heads. Whole-main-off separately zeros STAR MLP contribution. STAR is an independent multilayer FCN: PN uses domain moving moments, shared×domain affine scale plus additive shifts; every FCN matrix is shared⊙domain, biases additive; [256,128,1] main and [64,1] auxiliary logit network. Auxiliary receives the same fields including domain. PN/domain routing works on each domain subset of a mixed batch; this is a declared batching adaptation to retain paired populations. Single-task task0/task1 and two-head extension are separate models.

PEPNet follows paper equations 2–9: GateNU=2sigmoid(Linear(ReLU(Linear(prior)))); EP input is learned domain prior + detached general embedding; PP input is available user/item-side embeddings + detached EP output; per-layer gates scale tower inputs. Available KR schema has no user/item IDs, so use available profile/item fields as declared priors; do not fabricate unavailable industrial priors. Two towers [256,128,1]. EP-neutral and PP-neutral use coefficient1, independently and jointly.

HAMUR uses author public MLP [256,128], bottleneck32,k35, shared instance hypernetwork [64,k²] and residual adapter; reference retains public all-ones factors and public batch-statistic adapter normalization, including in eval. This differs from paper domain-specific factors and DN. Algebraic associative contractions avoid materializing B×128×32 matrices, checked against original forward/gradients. Record this as author-code-style, not exact paper reproduction. Additional paper-aligned variant, if needed, is separate. No-adapter, static-domain core and private hypernetwork are trainable structural controls. MMoE/PLE use repository audited cores. Expert-off is zero output without gate renormalization; private replacements supply legitimate trainable alternatives.
