## Evidence discipline

Publication metadata and abstracts do not substitute for full-text/code inspection. The research contribution must be framed against the actual mechanism after a complete follow-up review. Similarity is not automatic rejection; it is a reason to focus the claim on a testable difference. No performance result is claimed by this plan.
