## Evidence classifications

- STRONG: five-seed performance under the frozen protocol, or directly verified execution/identity fact.
- MEDIUM: repeatable but limited to tested version, dataset, or metric.
- WEAK: local probes, two-seed results, single-seed exploration, or mechanism interpretation not independently tied to performance.
- NOT_TRIGGERED / NOT_RUN / COST_BLOCKED: no negative performance evidence.

## Key unresolved gap

Historical work used (a) isotropic Gaussian perturbation, (b) task/label-conditioned sample or pair selection, and (c) parameter-gradient/momentum transformations. It has not yet directly tested task-specific adversarial perturbation of the input embedding with an explicit constraint that other-task outputs remain locally unchanged, trained through the original target BCE objective. This is the candidate gap, not a claim that all feature-space task perturbation is new in the literature.
