# Development decisions and bounded extensions

All188 initial formal/control runs complete regardless of these readings. The
preplanned32 P3/P4/P5 alternative runs also complete after their primary readouts;
no proxy/single-seed cancellation. These rules govern additional scientific
discrimination, not early termination of authorized budgets.

- P1 compares both regularized candidates with ERM, task masking and V-REx; a
  win over the deliberately incompatible global intervention is insufficient.
  If known-rule masking explains the entire benefit, do not claim a new stability
  algorithm. If a candidate has repeatable benefit beyond masking, investigate
  whether task1 information retention or optimization, rather than shift
  robustness, explains it before extending to another nuisance regime.
- P2 uses nested_axis to isolate joint likelihood and audit_fixed to control
  conventional anchored correction. A repeatable joint/axis advantage calls for
  a declared non-additive channel perturbation, with same-information controls,
  to test assumption sensitivity. A clean-oracle win alone proves nothing about
  identification or the proposed estimator. Report channel errors at selected
  and final stages alongside clean risks; do not equate rate recovery with AUC.
  Competing explanation: additive channel logits have three parameter paths per
  cell, so Adam's coordinate geometry can move rates faster than independent
  logits. If sharing appears beneficial, a bounded channel-rate learning-speed
  control may be necessary before attributing that benefit to information.
- P3 first reads rank_protected versus spectral-uniform on both datasets, then
  runs both dictionary mechanisms. Distinguish compressed-storage benefit from
  allocation-specific benefit and shared-dictionary benefit from task-specific
  coefficients. If one has repeatable same-budget gains that matter beyond its
  control, run a bounded optimizer extension on candidate/control. A lower loss
  but lower AUC is a trade-off requiring explicit scope, not automatic success.
- P4 reads interaction versus domain_mix/full_mix, then completes reliability
  versus confidence-off. A gain only over fixed mixing could be ordinary routing.
  Evaluate the effective subspace, source weights, replicated slope consistency
  and actual extra measurement time. Do not infer cheap recovery of interactions
  from a selected score if full learned mixing explains it at equal/lower cost.
  ML's smallest train-meta pool yields only75 rows/half, versus256 elsewhere;
  replicated-sign noise can therefore differ by probe size as well as true risk.
  This is not by itself proof that small domains need more or less sharing.
- P5 reads finite regret versus ordinary full mixing and train-meta utility, then
  completes leave-one-out coalition credit. A meaningful remaining endpoint
  advantage justifies a same-checkpoint decision comparison of finite functions
  and local utility. A theoretical permutation argument alone is insufficient
  evidence for an MDMT algorithm. Compare cost before invoking interpretability.
- P6 reads independent/positive/antithetic masks, adaptive copula and both control
  variate arms. If adaptive policy collapses to a fixed endpoint, record the
  equivalence rather than call the fixed-design effect adaptive intelligence.
  If prediction augmentation explains the gain, attribute it to the conventional
  component. A still-unresolved stochastic-noise mechanism may justify a smaller
  batch at equal full data/epoch budget, but not a blind batch-size grid.

Four-seed consistency, per-task/cell behavior, selected versus final, population
weights, baseline difficulty and cost determine evidence strength jointly. A
direction can be closed for the tested mechanism after two structural attempts
and strong controls, while its broader scientific problem remains open. A
literature-covered effect may be practically useful yet fail originality.

Every actual extension needs a numbered design/implementation/self-review record
and complete prescribed seeds before test freeze. No post-test method changes.
