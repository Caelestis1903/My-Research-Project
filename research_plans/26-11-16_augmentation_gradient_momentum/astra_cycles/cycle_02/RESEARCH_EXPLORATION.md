## 3. Three mathematically different candidates

| Candidate | Changed mathematical object | Competing explanations and predicted evidence | Cost and decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: NQR-025 | Replace 25% of a natural within-cell pair-completed BCE risk with a probability-difference quadratic risk; exact batch moments, no sampled pairs | Relative-score supervision adds information: NQR exceeds CLEAN and matched pointwise control. Generic squared-error regularization: pointwise control matches/exceeds NQR. Within-class compression is harmful: mean-gap ablation exceeds NQR. All three can fail against EMA. | One ordinary model forward/backward, O(B) reductions, no persistent additional tensors. **Selected** for a cheap, discriminating three-arm test. |
| B: local prediction-distribution consistency | C + eta/(2B) sum KL(Ber(stop(p_clean)) || Ber(p(e+r_adv))); r maximizes prediction KL, not target BCE | Output smoothness matters independently of BCE ascent: prediction KL and validation improve over equal-radius isotropic KL. If neither endpoint improves, local sensitivity was not limiting. Lower KL alone is insufficient. | Clean/finite-difference-direction/perturbed evaluations, roughly 3 forward passes and direction differentiation; radius, eta and masking choices need new design. **Unselected**: close to an already weak local-response story and costlier. |
| C: parameter-neighborhood sharpness (SAM family) | Minimize max_{||epsilon||2<=rho} C(theta+epsilon); first-order epsilon=rho*g/||g||, ordinary data gradient g | Parameter-neighborhood sharpness limits generalization: sharpness falls and EMA AUC improves versus equal-norm random parameter displacement. EMA already captures useful smoothing or parameter scaling dominates: no increment despite local loss change. | Two model forward/backward passes, temporary parameter displacement O(P), one restored-base Adam/EMA update; radius calibration and coupled-WD interaction unresolved. **Unselected**: not a parameter-noise rerun authorized by this bundle. |

They intervene on conditional prediction smoothness and parameter-neighborhood risk, respectively; neither is claimed globally new. No numeric tuning range or implementation authorization is assigned to B/C.

## 4. Why select A despite weak efficacy evidence

The strongest repeated recent observation is a gap between plausible local geometry and useful selected endpoints. A changes the output objective while preserving all model/data/optimizer/EMA operations, makes no perturbation, and adds no historical channel. Its controls expose specific reasons it might fail. The evidence supports running a falsifier, not expecting success. Historical PAIR already tested logistic logit ranking; exact non-equivalence is therefore required and documented, rather than pretending ranking is untried.

A is deliberately not a macro-AUC-consistent estimator: natural domain weights and opposite-label opportunity weights remain. It can underemphasize rare positive support. Reweighting cells/classes to repair a negative result is outside this cycle. Lambda=.25 is a single registered intervention budget, not an inferred optimum. Unit mixture coefficients do not equal identical gradient magnitudes or identical effective regularization under coupled Adam. This is measured and disclosed.
