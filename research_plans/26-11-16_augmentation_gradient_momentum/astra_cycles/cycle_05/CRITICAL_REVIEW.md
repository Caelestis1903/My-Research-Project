## Why this question warrants one complete cycle

C04 provides repeated task1 LL benefits under W−S but that contrast changed class/cell/task allocation. U holds all six cell masses and every task total fixed against W. Omitting U leaves that specific confound unresolved; adding only U permits a full five-seed class-policy intervention at half C04's new trajectory cost. No current evidence requires a parameter sweep, alternative backbone, extra dataset, task-only arm, model-state reconstruction or another Astra cycle.

An attractive result would be U preserving W-associated LL benefits while reducing KRd0t0 rank/loss harm. The design equally permits the opposite: class weighting may be the source of the LL improvement, and removing it may worsen both datasets. A cell-mass policy can remain harmful even when it is constant within a cell. The primary AUC threshold and complete matrix do not depend on those outcomes.

## Scientific limits that change interpretation

- **Global versus local mass.** U preserves W's exact cell/task/global masses, not natural unit mass and not class masses. It is not class-balanced. U−S still combines cell and task allocation. Do not label this a class-only explanation of the entire original W−S effect.
- **History and interaction.** W−U is an intervention on a coefficient policy followed by full source dynamics. It does not hold learned parameters or Adam moments fixed along the trajectory. Same-state diagnostic steps identify a local response only. `(W−S)=(W−U)+(U−S)` at endpoints is arithmetic, not an additive causal mediation model. S+R interaction remains unmeasured.
- **Predictor optimum.** Fixed-weight independent-cell BCE algebra concerns an idealized risk. Natural minibatch proportions are random and include each row's label, and M3oE shares parameters. U does not guarantee calibration or ranking improvement.
- **Prior evidence.** C04's KR LL and E50/d2 gains, TV MLγ.20, C01 KR IID2 and C03 KR LATE remain positive counterevidence. Prior task0 joint rank/loss cost does not recur uniformly. No universal augmentation, curriculum, momentum or difficult-domain failure claim follows.
- **Seed/statistical dependence.** C/W/S are numeric reused controls, not new independent runs. All cross-cycle work shares seeds and validation population; epochs, tasks, cells, diagnostics, identical prefixes and controls do not enlarge n=5. Report paired t4 descriptive intervals and raw signs; crossing intervals do not prove equality.
- **Efficacy versus mechanism.** U−C selected fixed EMA is the efficacy primary. U−S task1 LL and U−W KRd0t0 are registered mechanism directions. Report them even when the primary fails. No CI-exclusion or all-positive-seed eligibility gate is added; mean LL<0 remains separate from AUC qualification.
- **Size and difficulty.** Counts and baseline AUC/LL/seed SD are separate axes. ML smallest is best on baseline AUC; KR small domains disagree. No size manipulation is performed, so a main causal size explanation remains untested. A d0/d2 trade-off is not automatically a small/large trade-off.
- **Published baseline status.** C/W/S are internal matched controls on a fixed1 adaptation of published M3oE. Passing the internal screen does not show superiority to HyperGate/AutoIFS or a new SOTA result. Test/champion automation is absent.
