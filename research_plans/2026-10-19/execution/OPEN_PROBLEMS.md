# Open problems and one discriminating next experiment

These are limits of the completed prescribed experiment, not additional runs counted as completed and not an automatic extension of its matrix. Publication value and execution completion are separate decisions.

1. **Geometry versus gradient scale/gauge.** Coupled L2 suppresses the KR fixed product, but independent STAR escapes despite initially larger L2/data-gradient norm ratios. Layer depth/PN, embedding gradients, parameter scales, biases and bypass competition may jointly determine the outcome. The established scale-minimized sparse penalty does not prove nonlinear finite-time Adam dynamics.
2. **Positive decoupled shrink on ML.** At the frozen LR/WD, scalar float32 multiplication is identity. Global AdamW and Adam WD0 final model SHA match for all12 tested fixed/STAR pairs. The effect of a genuinely representable positive decoupled penalty remains NOT_ESTABLISHED; changing the coefficient or precision after outcomes would be a new experiment, not repair of these faithfully executed nominal recipes.
3. **Predictive harm and useful remedies.** Path restoration sometimes improves AUC but worsens LogLoss, and intervals versus global WD0/AdamW/PLE overlap zero. Active-path removal/retraining sometimes improves selected risk. A general harmful-failure interpretation and a general competitive rescue are not established.
4. **Independent confirmation.** Four seeds vary optimization on one fixed population; cells/tasks are correlated. The historical test population has repeatedly informed earlier N19 work and cannot be fresh confirmation. No novel broadly useful candidate was selected to justify automatic Ali-CCP/new-population method confirmation. Such confirmation remains necessary for a population-general performance claim.
5. **Model fidelity.** Public HAMUR mixed-batch BN/DN, shared/all-ones factors and eval batch dependence differ from strict paper definitions. Independent STAR/PEPNet and audited MMoE/PLE are declared public-data adaptations. Cross-paper failure/performance claims are not licensed by this matrix. No module-scale ranking across non-equivalent neutral interventions is valid.
6. **Task-sharing capacity.** G0MC correctly matches mean-task objective scaling but doubles backbones. Joint-versus-independent performance includes sharing and capacity differences, not a pure equal-capacity task-conflict estimate. Native single-task baselines must retain same-task comparisons.
7. **Information, replay and natural size.** G4 fixes exposure/update count in its matched schedule, but target resampling changes repetition/noise and uses one nested subset per fraction. The original largest domain remains largest at25%; this does not test the smallest-domain regime. Fewer unique rows hurt risk without changing the measured inactivity boundary. Size is relevant to risk but is not established as the main cause of inactivity; neither is universal size irrelevance established.
8. **Protocol bridge.** Original versus original_sample changes Equal/Sample weighting and overlapping auxiliary optimization together. The performance gap cannot be assigned uniquely to one component. New original cycles auxiliary training batches as the public source; historical N19's first-shuffle auxiliary batch differs. Neither setting is a literal industrial reproduction.
9. **Training stage and loss.** Final checkpoints often worsen despite growing path activity. Train-online risk uses changing within-epoch weights. LogLoss worsening establishes probability-risk deterioration, not a specific calibration decomposition or a proven label-shift cause. The constant-entropy diagnostic uses evaluation-cell prevalence and is not a deployable fitted constant model.
10. **Numerical/statistical scope.** Exact float32 prediction identity establishes inactivity on fixed probes/checkpoints, not a mathematical global zero map or irreversible absorbing state. Conditional row bootstrap reuses fixed predictions, including HAMUR batch semantics. Four-seed t intervals are descriptive, unadjusted for many comparisons and not independent-population confidence intervals.
11. **Cost interpretation.** Profiles cover supported dense operators on768-row meta batches; full-run FLOPs are shape-based estimates. CPU latency, process wall time and GPU readout timings are shared-host measurements. Frozen serialized parameters are not physical pruning; doubled backbones are not free. No production speedup is established.

The most discriminating next study is a **preregistered function-preserving scale-gauge intervention on a fresh temporal/user population**, using the same architecture, data, exposure and training budget: replace shared/domain factors by(c*s,v/c), verifying identical initial effective matrices/logits, then compare coupled-L2 Adam with the same Adam WD0 controls through complete training. Choose a small fixed set of gauges before results, retain four paired seeds and selected/final per-cell AUC/LogLoss, and record the same path and moment diagnostics. A representable AdamW arm may be specified up front if decoupled regularization is the question.

If collapse follows the factor gauge chiefly under coupled L2 while WD0 is stable, that would favor the regularizer/scale interaction over domain-size or dataset-identity explanations. Similar degradation under both would challenge that attribution and favor optimizer conditioning/competition or other representation effects. Matching the initial function prevents an additive-versus-product initialization change from answering the wrong question. This experiment is proposed, not executed or counted in the856-run matrix; uncertainty alone does not trigger another grid.
