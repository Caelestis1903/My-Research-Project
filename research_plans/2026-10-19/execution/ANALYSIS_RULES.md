# Pre-test analysis rules

Frozen before formal results are interpreted. No test values are used in these rules. The planned matrix, not apparent effect size, determines completion.

1. Unit of repeated performance evidence is the optimization seed2022–2025 on the fixed population. Describe95% t intervals (df3) as exploratory, not population confidence or multiplicity-adjusted proof. Probe conditional intervals resample rows inside each domain/task and do not inflate the number of seeds.
2. Separately report selected/final AUC, LogLoss, training-online loss/AUC, selection/development performance, every domain/task cell, original domain size, positive rates, constant-predictor skill and baseline quality. Original Equal+dual-optimizer results are a protocol comparison, not a pure architecture estimate. Single-task networks do not become one MDMT model by averaging their metrics.
3. Numerical inactivity is a scoped statement about repeated checkpoint sensitivity at float32 resolution with negligible effective product(s). Near-zero factors alone do not qualify. Zero modulation sensitivity does not imply all STAR output, biases, skip or downstream shared experts are inactive. PEP gate1, HAMUR adapter-off and expert-output0 are different estimands and remain separate.
4. Stronger evidence for decay attribution requires the local group interventions, same-data long retraining and common-state local counterfactuals to agree. Full-model AdamW alone does not isolate modulation decay. Common-state moments are held fixed for a local next-step question; they are not the long-run moments of the alternative optimizer.
5. Compare every G1 local candidate against both global WD0 and AdamW; compare G2/G3 with their native model and include strongest natural baselines. STAR+ additive fusion and known Hadamard sparsity geometry constrain novelty independently of outcome.
6. Quantify actual four-seed signs, per-cell effects and whether signs reverse between selected and final. A final-only gain is not a deployed selected-checkpoint gain. AUC and calibration losses can conflict; do not hide that conflict behind one composite metric.
7. G4 matched sampling preserves optimizer count, per-domain exposure/mixture and original evaluation. Its nested random target subset tests unique training size in one physical domain. Normal-epoch subsampling also changes mixture/update count, so that contrast alone is not a pure size effect. Natural domain sizes do not establish difficulty; baseline AUC/skill remain task- and model-specific empirical proxies.
8. Report a small-domain improvement/large-domain decline pattern only with original physical sizes and seed/cell signs. It is an observed trade-off, not proof size causes it. Compare controlled G4 evidence with natural-domain associations.
9. Evidence strength: STRONG only for narrowly scoped repeated direct numerical observations or converging controlled evidence; MEDIUM for consistent controlled fixed-population performance without independent confirmation; WEAK for mixed seeds, interval overlap, one architecture/population or unresolved controls. No broad out-of-population STRONG performance claim from four correlated fixed-population seeds.
10. Finish with stable phenomena, contradictions, competing explanations, strength/limits, role of size, trade-off, and one most discriminating further experiment. Do not automatically execute another grid merely because uncertainty remains. External confirmation/new population is justified only for a useful novel direction after development review; record whether it was available/needed and why.

Outputs distinguish four axes: structural scope; causal mechanism; selected-performance usefulness; prior-art coverage. A null result can complete the study, while a missing required run cannot. GO/CONDITIONAL/NO-GO applies to publication value, not whether the execution is complete.
