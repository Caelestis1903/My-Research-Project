## Scientific findings determining disposition

**STRONG, narrowly scoped:** fixed M3oE on KR with coupled-L2 Adam has exactly zero selected modulation sensitivity in all24 seed/domain/task probe cells. This concerns the effective input-weight modulation, not all main-path biases, the entire main network or downstream shared experts. Native original protocol is also strongly attenuated at selection (mean1.60e-7, max5.48e-6), then exactly zero in24/24 final cells; original_sample is approximately2.31e-9 at selection. Native independent STAR is a direct counterexample to universal STAR-family collapse: KR selected mean sensitivity .279/.257 for task0/1, with no zero cells, and substantial activity on ML. PEPNet gates, HAMUR residual adapters and MMoE/PLE shared experts remain active under their own, non-equivalent interventions.

**STRONG for local KR fixed-path attribution; limited generality:** removing coupled L2 on both factor groups, using local AdamW, global WD0 or global AdamW all preserves substantial KR fixed modulation. Selected means are .0316/.0353/.0283/.0299 respectively. Removing only shared or only domain-factor decay leaves much weaker paths (.0000286/.00000852 selected, approximately1e-6 final). Common-state initial hypothetical Adam-L2 steps shrink the KR shared norm by1.319 on average, whereas zero-L2/AdamW increases it by.0197; later historical moments can still shrink factors after changing the next-step rule. At collapsed selected states none of the three one-step rules immediately changes predictions. This is not a proof of irreversible collapse or a universal finite-time theorem. STAR's even larger initial decay/data-gradient norm ratios coexist with later active factors, falsifying a sufficient-condition claim based on that ratio alone.

**Relevant numerical boundary, resolved by measurement rather than retuning:** ML LR=.001 and nominal AdamW WD=1e-5 produce a float32 shrink multiplier of1.0. CPU and CUDA scalar-multiply checks change0/10,001 values; all12 matched fixed/STAR seed pairs have identical final model SHA and selected epoch for global AdamW versus global Adam WD0. Its outcome cannot identify the effect of a representable positive decoupled penalty. This is behavior of the frozen standard float32 recipe, not an implementation divergence to be silently corrected by a post-result change of LR/WD/precision. It constrains the claim; the separate coupled-L2 group interventions still answer the core attribution question. Evidence is in DECAY_ROUNDING_CHECK.json.

**MEDIUM for particular fixed-population effects, WEAK for a broadly useful rescue:** KR fixed local AdamW selected AUC improves .007599 (4/4 positive; descriptive95% seed interval [.003292,.011906]) versus native Adam, while LogLoss worsens .001303. **Competing pathway explanations:** removing the KR fixed bypass restores selected modulation (.0418 mean) but reduces selected AUC .089489 (4/4 worse);12/24 final probe cells subsequently have zero modulation. Merely balancing initial main/bypass RMS still gives exactly zero KR modulation at selection and final. Additive factors stay active but have mixed fixed-model selected gains, while ML STAR additive loses .033956/.023415 AUC for the two tasks under Adam (all seeds worse). Additive changes effective initialization and induced regularization as well as conditioning. No-modulation retraining can improve ML STAR selected AUC and LogLoss while its native modulation remains active. These observations separate functional activity, fixed-state usefulness and retraining usefulness.

**Cross-model controls:** MMoE/PLE private replacements generally lose KR performance; matched PLE private Adam loses .020840 AUC, all four seeds. ML HAMUR adapter removal has large gains over its own weak native baseline (task0 Adam+.044933, LogLoss-.452883), but still trails the same-task fixed baseline. Joint STAR versus normalized independent-backbone control changes sign by population (KR Adam-.006299, ML+.005136); the latter control doubles capacity. Universal destructive task sharing is not established.

**Size, difficulty and stage:** G4 matched exposure/update controls reduce selected AUC with fewer unique target rows in both populations, including active-path STAR. Fixed25% target-domain AUC changes are KR-.043395 and ML-.025246, all seeds negative; the other domains also decline (KR-.016192, ML-.014979). Thus unique information/replay is relevant to risk in this intervention. Nevertheless all KR fixed size controls retain exactly zero selected modulation; all ML controls remain nonzero. The target remains the largest physical domain even at25%, and only one nested subset per fraction is used. All16 long runs select the same checkpoints as the original500-update runs; their final performance deteriorates substantially. Natural domain size is not a demonstrated main explanation of inactivity, nor a synonym for difficulty. A stable small-domain gain/large-domain decline trade-off is not established.

## Decision and test release boundary

Execute the entire frozen selected/final test readout for all856 models, with zero training updates and the already specified neutral interventions. Keep the four seeds, cells, tasks, selected/final and AUC/LogLoss separate. No model/optimizer/WD/LR/architecture/selection changes are selected from these outcomes. Test population is the repeatedly used historical N19 population, so this readout is exploratory and cannot become fresh population confirmation.

Publication disposition before test: **NO-GO for a universal conditional-path-collapse claim or a novel general rescue algorithm. CONDITIONAL for a carefully bounded empirical mechanism study.** Structural universality and general selected-performance benefit are NOT_ESTABLISHED. KR fixed coupled-L2 suppression is well supported within this implementation; known AdamW/WD0, Hadamard sparsity geometry and STAR+ alternatives create substantial prior-art coverage. Large improvements over a weak HAMUR baseline do not establish competitive method value. Specific useful effects remain development findings, not independently confirmed benefits.

No extra seed grid or external population is launched: there is no selected novel algorithm with robust benefit against the strong controls that justifies a new method-confirmation stage. This is the preregistered conditional externality decision after the whole matrix, not proxy-based cancellation of any required experiment. Generalization and genuinely positive decoupled regularization on ML remain explicitly unconfirmed. The best further falsification study is an independently sampled population with identical initial effective function and explicit representable coupled/decoupled factor penalties, preserving other-parameter optimizer histories initially and then running complete matched-budget training. This would separate regularizer geometry/gradient scale from dataset label and size explanations; it is a future proposal, not an unreported completed experiment.

Four axes: structural scope—bounded M3oE protocol phenomenon, broad universality NOT_ESTABLISHED; mechanism—strong local causal evidence, general theorem NOT_ESTABLISHED; selected causal value—heterogeneous, broad usefulness NOT_ESTABLISHED; novelty—existing methods/theory cover the tested remedies, new algorithm novelty NOT_ESTABLISHED. Final interpretation must retain contradictory test evidence rather than changing these frozen choices.
