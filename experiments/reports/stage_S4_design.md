## Scope and design decision

This specification covers **S4 only**. Its research object is the M3OE, KuaiRand `natural`, physical domain `tab1` (local domain 1), two tasks `click` and `long_view`, under the existing Equal objective. It is a mechanism diagnosis, not a new recommendation method. No other dataset, rotation, backbone, objective, seed, donor search, hyperparameter search, Momentum, EMA, curriculum, or later stage is authorized here.

**Design decision: GO with bounded identification.** A same-state counterfactual Adam calculation and two longitudinal controls can distinguish a simple gradient-norm explanation from effects that survive step-size matching. They cannot hold an entire Adam moment history identical while changing gradient composition during training: Adam's moments are functions of past gradients. Consequently a positive S4 result may support *direction/composition plus its induced optimizer history*; it cannot alone prove geometric gradient conflict or attribute all AUC change to direction rather than state. The reporting rules below enforce that boundary. If the counterfactual step cannot be computed and validated against the installed Adam implementation, this design is **NO-GO for formal training** pending a new S4 amendment and reviewer approval.

## 1. Research question

Can the S3 Equal `tab1` rescue be reproduced by keeping the FULL shared-gradient direction while matching either the target-only shared-gradient norm or the same-state counterfactual target-only Adam update magnitude? If not, is there evidence that the non-target gradient's direction or the optimizer history it creates contributes? What cost appears in the four non-target cells, and does the pattern survive matched training epochs?

## 2. Competing hypotheses

- **H-M (gradient magnitude):** The target rescue follows the size of the shared gradient. Scaling FULL gradients to the target-only norm reproduces most of the rescue.
- **H-U (applied update magnitude/Adam conditioning):** The rescue follows the size of the Adam parameter move. Matching the same-state target-only Adam step magnitude while preserving the FULL gradient and its Adam moments reproduces most of it, even if raw-gradient norm matching does not.
- **H-C (composition/history contribution):** The target-only arm remains materially better than both magnitude controls, and same-state probes show that FULL updates are less favorable to the target loss at equal update norm. This supports composition or its induced optimizer trajectory, not a proof of pure conflict.
- **H-K (checkpoint/convergence):** Selected-checkpoint differences do not persist at common epochs and the fixed final epoch.
- **H-0:** The S3 rescue fails to reproduce, effects are seed-unstable, the two tasks disagree, controls are not matched, or contrasts are too small for a useful mechanism claim.

Each is falsifiable. In particular, a FULL-direction control that reproduces target-only performance weakens the directional account. An unfavorable target-only result weakens the S3 generalization.

## 5. Mathematical and algorithmic definition

For each train batch of size `B`, use the S3 task-mean BCE per example `ell_i`, and set

`L = B^-1 Σ_i w[d_i] ell_i`, `L* = B^-1 Σ_{i:d_i=1} w[1] ell_i`, `L_non=L-L*`.

For a shared group `G` (`C` at each main step, `A_arch` at each architecture step), flatten its named tensors in a fixed order and calculate from **one stochastic model forward**:

`g_F = ∇_G L`, `g_T = ∇_G L*`, `g_non = g_F-g_T`.

Do not take additional training forwards merely to obtain the alternatives; this M3OE forward changes `tau` and may use Gumbel randomness. Preserve `None`/zero gradient semantics exactly as the S3 audited partition and the Adam implementation require. The group norm is `||g||_2 = sqrt(Σ_tensor ||g_tensor||_2²)` in float64 for audit; the applied tensors keep the normal training dtype.

The shared gradient rules are:

- `F`: `g_applied=g_F`.
- `T`: `g_applied=g_T`.
- `N`: `g_applied = r_N g_F`, with `r_N=||g_T||_2/||g_F||_2`. Thus `||g_applied||_2=||g_T||_2` within numeric tolerance while FULL direction is preserved whenever both norms are positive. Feed this scaled gradient to ordinary Adam, including ordinary coupled weight decay.
- `U`: feed **unscaled** `g_F` to ordinary Adam. At the same pre-step parameters and optimizer state `s`, compute the counterfactual target-only Adam delta `Δ_T^cf = AdamDelta(θ,s,g_T)` and the actual FULL Adam delta `Δ_F = AdamDelta(θ,s,g_F)`, including coupled decay, bias correction, epsilon, parameter-specific step counts, and the distinct main/architecture learning rates. Set `r_U=||Δ_T^cf||_2/||Δ_F||_2` over the shared group and apply `θ_G ← θ_G + r_U Δ_F`; commit Adam's **FULL-gradient** moment/state transition exactly once. Private parameters undergo their ordinary FULL Adam step, without `r_U`. The actual shared delta must match the counterfactual delta's group norm within tolerance. `U` does not claim to match the target-only arm's *historical* moment state.

`AdamDelta` is a read-only, side-effect-free calculation from a snapshot of current tensors and optimizer state. The coder must independently validate it against a disposable clone of the installed `torch.optim.Adam` on representative named tensors, including first step, nonzero moments, zero gradient, weight decay, and architecture optimizer. A failure is a formal-run blocker. `U` must have one normal `optimizer.step()` per batch/architecture batch; adjusting the shared parameter delta after that step is allowed, provided moments, step counters, private deltas, and weight decay are audited unchanged. The scaling is a diagnostic parameter-move intervention, not a claim that it is exactly a standard Adam gradient step.

If `||g_F||=0<||g_T||` for `N`, or `||Δ_F||=0<||Δ_T^cf||` for `U`, the direction-preserving control is undefined: fail closed, record the key and request a reviewed S4 amendment. If both norms are zero, use scale 1 and record the zero/zero case. If only the target norm is zero, scale 0 and record it; if this occurs in over 1% of main shared steps in any arm/seed, mark that arm non-identifying because its FULL direction was removed too often. Do not silently clamp or cap either ratio. Require finite parameters, gradients, moments, and scales.

### Same-state directional and conditioning probe

At the **first main minibatch of each epoch**, for every arm and seed, reuse the actual forward and gradient decomposition and calculate virtual `Δ_F`, `Δ_T^cf`, and `Δ_N^cf` from the same pre-step model/Adam state without committing additional updates or changing RNG/tau. Record (a) `cos(g_F,g_T)` on `C`, (b) cosine of `Δ_F` and `Δ_T^cf`, (c) target-loss first-order directional quantities `-g_T·Δ_F`, `-g_T·Δ_T^cf`, and `-g_T·(r_U Δ_F)` with the sign convention “positive predicts target-loss decrease,” and (d) raw gradient and Adam-delta norms plus weight-decay contribution. Architecture probes occur at the single architecture update each epoch. Probe results are local diagnostics; they do not replace held-out test AUC and cannot establish that a one-step training-loss derivative caused the final AUC.

## 6. Comparisons, ablations, and predeclared decisions

For each seed, task, and checkpoint let `Y_X` be test AUC of arm `X`, and define `R_T=Y_T-Y_F`, `R_N=Y_N-Y_F`, `R_U=Y_U-Y_F`, `D_N=Y_T-Y_N`, `D_U=Y_T-Y_U`. Calculate the same deltas for LogLoss, with lower being better. The six pairwise contrasts `T-F`, `N-F`, `U-F`, `T-N`, `T-U`, `N-U` must be emitted for **all six cells**, without selecting by sign. Selected checkpoints are selected independently by the frozen validation rule; common-epoch comparisons use identical epoch numbers. Report four individual seeds, arithmetic mean, sample SD, sign count, worst seed, and all five common-epoch mean/sign patterns. Do not use a p-value or a threshold as proof of significance with four seeds.

Predeclared practical AUC threshold is `0.003`; `0.005` is a strong single-contrast magnitude. First require fresh reproduction: both `tab1` tasks must have mean `R_T >= +0.003`, positive `R_T` in at least 3/4 seeds, favorable mean LogLoss, and positive `R_T` at at least 3/5 common epochs including epoch 49. Otherwise S4 mechanism classification is **unresolved**, even if a control looks favorable.

- **Magnitude-compatible:** for both tasks, `R_N` or `R_U` reaches at least 80% of `R_T` at selected and final checkpoints, with at least 3/4 seeds positive, while the matching `D_N` or `D_U` is below `+0.003` in mean. Name which control passed; do not assert equivalence or necessity. If `U` passes and `N` fails, applied move/Adam conditioning is the better account than raw-gradient norm alone.
- **Composition/history-supported:** for both tasks, selected and final mean `D_N` **and** `D_U` are at least `+0.003`, each is positive in at least 3/4 seeds, and each remains positive at at least 3/5 common epochs including epoch 49. Same-state probes must show a consistent target-favorable direction for `Δ_T^cf` versus the equal-norm FULL update in at least 3/4 seeds when aggregated over the 50 fixed probe steps. If probe and AUC disagree, call the mechanism unresolved.
- **Mixed/unresolved:** all other patterns, including opposite tasks, unstable seeds, LogLoss deterioration, late-stage reversals, or failed matching. The two controls may each explain different portions; report both without forcing a binary verdict.

The 80% criterion is a descriptive sufficiency check relative to the fresh S4 effect, not a formal equivalence margin. If target-only improves tab1 but harms non-target cells, report the exact trade-off; neither magnitude-compatible nor composition/history-supported authorizes presenting the arm as safe deployment.

## 9. Two-process formal run and recovery plan

After coder implementation and independent reviewer `PASS`, or `PASS WITH FIXES` with all blocking fixes rechecked, freeze the 16-key manifest, source hashes, and pair schedule. Run exactly **two independent Python training processes concurrently** in each wave, each with isolated attempt/output directories on the same audited `cuda:0` protocol. Fixed resource waves: `(F,T)` for seeds 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, then `(N,U)` for those same seeds: eight two-process waves, with no overlapping waves or more than two live training processes. Scientific pairing is within seed across four arms, independent of launch partners. This schedule permits early F/T replication review before the control waves, but no analysis or redesign between waves; a failed replication still completes the frozen matrix unless a technical gate fails.

Use append-only attempt directories. Validate both wave members before atomic promotion. On process crash or artifact rejection, retain failed attempts and stdout, terminate/confirm both wave processes, review the specific failure, and retry the entire two-member wave from epoch 0 at most once if it is a transient execution fault and source/config are unchanged. Never resume a checkpoint, repair one member silently, replace a seed, or overwrite canonical evidence. A scientific null is not a reason to retry. ## 11. Falsification and limits

H-C is weakened if a FULL-direction magnitude control reaches the target-only rescue on both tasks and stages while its matching audit passes. H-M is weakened if `N` fails despite accurate gradient-norm matching. H-U is weakened if `U` fails despite accurate applied-step matching. S3's access explanation is weakened if fresh `T-F` fails on either task, if it occurs only at selected checkpoints, or if AUC/LogLoss disagree materially. An apparent target gain accompanied by non-target losses falsifies any claim of general improvement. No outcome in this matrix establishes universal M3OE behavior, domain-size causality, or pure geometric conflict. If both controls fail but probes do not show a consistent target-favorable directional difference, report unresolved composition versus optimizer-history dependence and stop this stage without retrofitting a preferred explanation.
